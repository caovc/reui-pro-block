# Bricklane branding and demo artwork

Bricklane is an independent recreation of the public ReUI Pro Blocks and Templates previews. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by ReUI or KeenThemes.

- The original Bricklane mark uses three offset bricks to form an abstract B. Its source is `src/components/brand-mark.tsx`; the matching favicon is `public/favicon.svg`.
- `src/lib/demo-artwork.ts` contains original SVG portraits, dashboard studies, and typographic integration badges. These replace ReUI-hosted avatars, decorative screenshots, and SVG logos extracted from preview DOM.
- Template source downloads include these local modules through their dependency graph, so the replacement artwork works without fetching ReUI media.
- Official reference links and catalog names remain to identify the layouts being recreated. Original names and comparison links are not the site's own brand.
- Official free ReUI components are still used under their MIT license. Preserve the component copyright and license notices. Licensed third-party fonts and stock photos retain their existing attribution records.
