# Cohort Time fidelity ledger

Updated 26 October 2026. This milestone covers **Time off** and **Timesheets** in the independent Bricklane Cohort recreation. It does not claim completion of the other template pages or pixel identity with the reference.

## Reference evidence

The public [Time off](https://cohort-hr.reui.io/time/time-off) and [Timesheets](https://cohort-hr.reui.io/time/timesheets) pages were explored in an independent headless Chromium session. The session waited for the completed demo sign-in redirect and `networkidle` before interacting. Screenshots and DOM text were recorded at 1440 × 1000 and 390 × 844. Reference media was viewed for comparison only; no original portraits, logos, paid screenshots or extracted SVGs were added to the application or download.

## Actual component reuse

- Official free ReUI EventCalendar, content and navigation components: the actual calendar engine and views, without a parallel custom calendar implementation.
- Official free `CalendarSelect`, adapted from `c-calendar-8`, for the mini-calendar, leave dates and selected week; official TimePicker for leave times.
- Official free Timeline for the day-by-day list, and NumberField through the existing `NumericField` Pro Block helper for editable hours.
- Official free [`c-progress-8`](https://reui.io/r/radix-nova/c-progress-8.json) independently colored Progress composition for controlled day and target meters. The fetched example was read before migration; colors, labels and controlled values are domain extensions.
- Existing project Pro Blocks `DomainRecordGrid`, `GridFrame`, `Identity` and `TinyAvatar`: actual ReUI DataGrid/Frame composition and original local portraits. Existing `Tone` uses ReUI Badge. Existing Select, Popover and Sheet plumbing remains shared with the family.

The leave model, per-week hours and decisions, application filters and target thresholds are domain composition. No official free calendar, timeline or grid capability was reimplemented. ReUI MIT notices, Inter OFL and third-party attribution are retained in the exported project.

## Remaining differences and limits

Bricklane's original brand mark and local demo portraits intentionally differ from the official brand and photos. Shared family sidebar/top-bar spacing and some icon or small geometric details still differ. All calendar views, drag/resizing, all filter combinations and browsers other than Chromium have not been exhaustively checked.

Cohort People still needs a separate View profile surface, its Import flow and further manager-action parity. The public Security confirmation gate prevents inspection of privileged reference security states. Unrelated Atlas, Verve, Miro and Commerce differences remain outside this milestone. Push success must not be described as a confirmed preview deployment.
