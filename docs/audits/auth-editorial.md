# Auth 6 and 8: editorial compositions

The public Auth6/Auth8 standalone previews were compared at desktop size before implementation. These entries previously fell through the generic auth family. They now have separate compositions rather than a title and background variation of that family.

The official free Frame and Alert implementations already existed and were inspected before this pass. `PasswordAuthForm`, customer marks, and policy dialogs are shared with the previously migrated auth entries. There is no duplicate handwritten frame, status panel, input implementation, or photograph rendering primitive. REUI has no free complete editorial authentication block or photo-overlay composition; the grid, column proportions, imagery placement and backdrop CSS are the consumer composition around those actual primitives.

Both new photos are packaged local files with provenance in `src/blocks/application/assets/auth-editorial/ATTRIBUTION.md`. They are free substitutions rather than downloaded paid assets. Demo prose is independently written; no photographic subject is identified as an endorsing customer.
