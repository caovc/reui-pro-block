# Original business template artwork

`product-0.svg` through `product-7.svg` and `store-study-0.svg` through `store-study-2.svg` are original Bricklane illustrations, authored from basic SVG geometry for this repository on 2026-10-23. They are distributed under the repository license. No ReUI preview image, avatar, screenshot or extracted path is included.

Product illustrations intentionally differ from the upstream photography while retaining the thumbnail sizes, neutral backgrounds and content hierarchy. Profile illustrations come from the repository’s original `demoAvatar` helper.

ReUI free component source and font licenses are retained separately in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md and the font asset directories.
